About Me

I am a Communication and Public Relations professional with over three years of experience in corporate communication, public relations, digital communication and stakeholder engagement.

Currently working as a Communication Officer at Karatina University, I support the University's communication and visibility through content development, social media management, media relations, event communication, branding, photography and stakeholder communication.

My work allows me to combine strategy and creativity — from planning communication around an event, to creating the content, capturing the moments and ensuring the story reaches the right audience.
